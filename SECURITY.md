# Security

DigiLog keeps all data on your device. It has no account and no server, and it never sends your data anywhere. Even so, a bug could expose or damage that data, for example through a backup file or by losing entries.

## Reporting a problem

**Please don't open a public issue for a security problem.** Report it privately instead: go to the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**.

Please include:

- what the problem is and what it could let someone do
- steps to reproduce it
- the release you installed (for example v1.1.0) and your Android version

You should get a reply within a week. Once a fix is released, the report can be made public, and you'll be credited unless you'd rather not be.

## Supported versions

Only the [latest release](https://github.com/GoutamkumarKoppolu/DigiLog/releases/latest) gets fixes. Updating installs over the old app and keeps your data.
